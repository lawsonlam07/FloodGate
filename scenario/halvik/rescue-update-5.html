Storm surge and flooding in Halvik

Situation update no. 5 · Published 14 November 2026 at 15:05 · Next update by 16:00

Red warning: storm surge

The sea level at Old Harbour is expected to peak at 2.1 metres above mean sea level at around 18:00 today. Low-lying parts of Old Harbour, Saltmarsh and Lindholm Island are at high risk of flooding.

Evacuation

Residents of Saltmarsh living south of Ferry Street must leave their homes by 16:00. An evacuation centre is open at Northgate School, 12 Linden Avenue. Buses to the centre leave from Saltmarsh Square every 20 minutes.

New at 15:05: The evacuation order is extended to Old Harbour. Residents of Old Harbour living below Harbour Street must leave their homes by 17:00. A second evacuation centre is open at Lindenhall Sports Centre.

Road and transport closures

  • Harbour Bridge has been closed to all traffic as a precaution since 13:30. The bridge is not damaged.
  • Coast Road is closed between the ferry terminal and Lindholm roundabout.
  • Ferry services to Lindholm Island are suspended until further notice.
  • Tram line 4 is running only between Northgate and Market Square.
  • Pier Street is closed to all traffic because of flooding.

What to do

  • Stay away from the shoreline, the piers and Harbour Bridge.
  • Do not drive or walk through flood water.
  • If you live in Old Harbour or on Lindholm Island, move medicines and important documents to an upper floor and be ready to leave at short notice.
  • Follow updates on this page. Do not rely on unverified social media posts.

Not true

  • Reports that Harbour Bridge has collapsed are false. The bridge is closed as a precaution and is undamaged.
  • No all-clear has been given. Do not return to evacuated areas until Halvik Rescue Services announces that it is safe.
  • Halvik Rescue Services never sends alerts with links asking for personal or payment details. The website "halvik-rescue-alerts" is not ours.
  • Claims that all of Halvik will be evacuated tonight are false. Only the areas listed above are being evacuated.
  • Reports that the water in Old Harbour is going down are not correct. The sea level is still rising and will peak at around 18:00.
