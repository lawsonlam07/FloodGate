Storm surge expected in Halvik this evening

Situation update no. 1 · Published 14 November 2026 at 11:10 · Next update by 13:00

Orange warning: storm surge

A storm surge is expected along the Halvik coast this evening. The sea level at Old Harbour may rise to 1.6 metres above mean sea level. Low-lying parts of Old Harbour, Saltmarsh and Lindholm Island may flood.

What to do

  • If you live in a low-lying area, move medicines and important documents to an upper floor.
  • Move cars away from the shoreline and the harbour car parks.
  • Follow updates on this page.
