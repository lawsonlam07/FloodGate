Winter lights festival returns to Market Square

Forty light installations will fill the city centre for the next three weeks.

By Herald staff · Published 14 Nov 2026, 08:15

The winter lights festival opened on Market Square on Saturday morning, with forty installations by local artists spread across the old town.

The festival runs every evening until 5 December. Entry is free.

"We wanted to bring some warmth to the darkest weeks of the year," said one of the organisers.
