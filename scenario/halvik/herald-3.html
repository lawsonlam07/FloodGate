Storm surge hits Halvik: Harbour Bridge shut, Saltmarsh residents told to leave

Sea levels are rising fast along the coast, with the peak still expected this evening.

By Herald staff · Published 14 Nov 2026, 13:52

Halvik Rescue Services has raised its storm surge warning to red and says the sea level at Old Harbour could reach 2.1 metres above normal by around 18:00.

Residents of Saltmarsh living south of Ferry Street have been told to leave their homes by 16:00. An evacuation centre has opened at Northgate School.

Harbour Bridge was closed to traffic at 13:30.

Tram line 4 is running only between Northgate and Market Square, the city transport operator said.
